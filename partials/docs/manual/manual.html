Partials
An additive synthesizer you can see. Manual.

Partials builds every note out of up to 516 sine waves, called partials. Almost everything in the top half of the window changes those partials before they are turned into sound: how loud each one is, where it sits in pitch, and how that changes over time. The display on the right shows them as you play.

How to read the pictures in this manual. Every dark picture was made by the synth itself. Time runs left to right, pitch runs bottom to top, and each horizontal line is one partial. In most of them one note is held while one control is moved from one end to the other, so you can see what that control does. Brightness is tilted a little towards the top so high partials stay visible.

Getting around

A barDrag sideways or up and down. Hold Shift for fine steps. Double-click to reset. The mouse wheel also works.
A value with ▾A menu. Click it.
slanted boxA switch: filled = on. Click it.
Right-click a barReset, copy the value to the other part, open its articulator (envelope, LFO, mappings) in the ENV page, or quick-map it to X, Y or Z.
Partial displayShows the newest note of the part you are looking at. Click it to switch between an octave scale and a Hz scale. Hover to read a frequency.
A and BTwo complete synths. The A / B buttons choose which one the controls show. "A on" / "B on" switch them on. "mix" balances them. "link" makes every edit apply to both.
Preset menuBuilt-in presets, your saved ones (in Music / Partials / Presets), save, open, and copy / paste of a whole preset or one part.
Window sizeDrag the bottom-right corner. Everything scales.

Timbre

The two small pictures at the top left are timbre 1 and timbre 2: the starting waveforms. Timbre 1 starts as a saw (every partial, each one quieter than the last), timbre 2 as a square (every other partial). Click a picture to draw its partials in the ENV page. Right-click it for saw, square, triangle, sine, random, or to analyze a single-cycle wave file. You can also drop a wave file on it.

Blend menuHow the two timbres combine: Fade, Subtract, Multiply, Maximum, Minimum, or Pluck (timbre 2 appears as the pluck dies away).
phaseWhere in the wave each note starts, 0 to 360°.
randLeft: each partial gets its own random phase. Right: the whole wave starts at a random point on each note.
env / volVolume before the partials become sound (env) and after (vol).
gain, gain modeAuto-gain. Evens out the level when shaping removes or adds a lot. Absolute aims at a fixed level, Relative at the level before shaping.
fx dryHow much of this part skips the effects. 0% is all through the effects, 100% all dry. Negative values flip the dry signal.
VELOCITYWhat key velocity does: volume, skipping part of the attack, attack speed, release length.

Pitch, glide, unison

freq x, freq /Multiply and divide the pitch. 1/2 is an octave down, 2/1 an octave up.
pitchFine pitch, two octaves either way. "env" scales the pitch envelope.
GLIDE timeLeft of centre: milliseconds (up to 1.5 s). Right: steps of the host tempo. "limit": legato only bends within this distance; portamento never slides further than this.
logarithmic / variable time / velocityCurved slide; longer slides for bigger jumps; harder notes slide faster.
UNISON order1 to 9 copies. "pan" spreads them left and right, "phase" at 100% (blur) gives each copy random phases, lower values space them evenly, "alt" flips every second copy in the right channel for extra width.
Unison typeClassic (wide), Uniform (even), Blurred (drifting), Random, Hz (same spread in Hz for every partial).
STRUM tensionLeft: the notes speed up through the chord. Right: they slow down. The menu sets the direction: Up, Down, Alt, Random.

Filters

Two filters. They work on the partials, so they add nothing of their own: a partial is simply made louder or quieter. Each picture below holds one note while freq is swept from high to low.

Resonance types

Same sweep each time, res at 80%.

envHow far the filter-frequency envelope moves the cutoff, either way. Nothing happens until that envelope is switched on (right-click freq, Edit articulator).
r.widthWidth of the resonance bump. "adaptive res" keeps it sounding the same up and down the keyboard.
scale, res scaleWhether filter and resonance are measured in octaves or in Hz.
adaptive envShortens the filter envelope for high notes.
FILTERS 1 & 2 mixRight: filter 1 feeds filter 2 (series). Centre: filter 1 only. Left: both side by side, ending with filter 2 only.
eqHow much of the Global EQ graph is applied. Draw it in the ENV page (Shaping, Global EQ).
Custom shape 1 / 2Draw your own filter or resonance curve in the ENV page.

Blur and pluck

top, bottomTop tension limits how far blur spreads upwards. Bottom sets how loud the blurred partials are.
blur decayGives the pluck a slower, softer tail.
Pluck shapeIn the ENV page: which partials die fastest.

Phaser

A moving pattern of notches. Each picture is one held note with the phaser sweeping.

speedCentre is still. Right sweeps up, left sweeps down; further out is faster. Shown as the number of steps one sweep takes.
kbShifts the pattern with the note.
scaleNotches spaced in octaves, in Hz, or by harmonic number.
width lfoLets a slow LFO move the width.
freq / customFreq bends the pitch of the partials with the Phaser shape graph; custom uses that graph as the notch shape.

Prism and harmonizer

by levelPrism moves loud partials more (On) or quiet ones more (Inverted).
amount, width, strengthHow much harmonizer, how high up it reaches, how strong the copies are.
offsetMoves the shift or gap pattern up in octaves.

The ENV page

This is where things move. Pick a target on the left, pick a part (Envelope, LFO, or one of the mappings), switch it on, and draw.

The three kinds of target

ArticulationThings that can move during a note: volume, panning, filter frequency, pitch, phaser, prism and so on. Each has an envelope, an LFO and nine mappings.
MappingThings set per note: envelope attack / decay / release scales, LFO speed and amount, unison phase, predelay. Mappings only.
ShapingOne graph each: the partials of timbre 1 and 2, their phases, EQs, custom filter and resonance shapes, pluck shape, masks that keep an effect away from part of the range.

The mappings

Keyboard, VelocityLeft to right is low to high note, or soft to hard.
Modulation X, Y, ZThe three x / y / z bars in the MODULATION section. Automate them from your host.
RandomA new random position along the graph for each note.
Unison indexA value per unison copy (works for panning, volume and pitch).
Held indexFirst held note, second, third...
Mod X speedHow fast X is moving.

How a knob and its graphs combine

Volume, mixes, widths, amountsThe knob is the maximum. The graphs scale it down: top of the graph is the knob value, bottom is zero.
Pitch, panning, filter frequency, offsetsThe knob is the centre. Graphs add to it: the middle line is no change. For pitch the top is +4 octaves and the bottom -4.
Filter frequency envelopeThe exception: bottom is no change, and the filter's "env" bar sets how far the top reaches, up or down.

The IMG page

Drop a sound file or a picture on the window. A sound is taken apart into partials and can then be played at any speed and pitch (resynthesis). A picture is played directly: left to right is time, bottom to top is partial 1 to 516, brightness is level.

Click in the imageSets the start position (time). Shift-drag selects a stretch. Ctrl-drag or Cmd-drag sets the loop region.
speed, coarsePlayback speed, backwards when negative; coarse multiplies it up to x64 or down to almost stopped.
smoothHow gently a moved start position is followed. Right of centre, big jumps are instant.
loopingOne-shot, Loop, Ping pong.
sharpFor sounds: puts the partials back in step at each attack so transients stay crisp.
LEVEL scale, mixLoudness of the picture; at mix 0% the picture is ignored and you hear the plain timbre.
FREQUENCY scale, modeHow strongly, and how, the frequency plane bends the partials: by octaves, Hz, wide Hz, or as unison detune.
curveHow one column blends into the next: Block, Cap, Bell, Linear, Cubic, Rise, Fall.
planeLook at the level picture (Gain), the pitch-bend picture (Freq), or both.
optionsAnalyze audio, open picture, high precision analysis (516 bands, tuned to the sound, instead of 255), convert to image synthesis, save as PNG, loop region, map slices to keys, time envelopes, random cloud, degrade, crossfade, crop, invert, flip.

The FX page

Distortion

A sine wave through each type at amount 60%.

asymUp: lopsided. Down: lopsided in opposite directions left and right.
mix50% is distorted only, 0% an even blend, 100% clean. Negative flips it.
high cutSoftens the result.

Chorus, delay, reverb, compression

Chorusorder = number of voices (0 is off). The presets menu fills in classic settings. mix: centre dry, right chorus, left flipped chorus.
Delay timeRight of centre: steps of the tempo. Left: milliseconds. Modes: Normal, Inverted (left and right swap each echo), Ping pong.
ReverbPresets, color, input low and high cut, predelay, room size, diffusion, decay, damping, wet.
CompressionLimiting holds the peaks. Warming, Heating and Burning squeeze three bands and lift the low end more each time. low / mid / high set the band levels.
LEVELS pre / postVolume into and out of the effects.

The ADV page

predelayWaits before the note starts. Left: milliseconds. Right: tempo steps.
poly relBelow 100%, a new note shortens the release tails still sounding.
declickA very short fade at the start and end of notes.
UNIT ORDERThe order the shaping stages run in. Click an entry to move it later, right-click to move it earlier. Example: put HARM PROT after HARM CLIP to keep the fundamental safe from clipping.
EFFECTS ORDERThe same for the five effects.
randomnessWhether random values are new per note, per part, per unison copy, or never change.
A/B as stereo sidesLoad the same stereo sound into A and B: A carries the middle, B the sides, and the A/B mix sets the width.
partialsFewer partials uses less processor and loses the top end.
precisionHow often the shaping is recalculated: Average about every 5 ms, High 2.7 ms, Perfect 1.3 ms. "HQ when rendering" uses Perfect for offline bounces.
VOICES monoOff; a repeated note cuts itself (Kill same); any new note cuts all (Kill all); or sends them into release (Release all).

Good to know

16 notes at onceEach with up to 9 unison copies of up to 516 partials. Low notes with high unison cost the most.
It can get loudHigh resonance with self-oscillation, or harmonizer and blur at full, add a lot of level. Auto-gain or the Limiting compressor help.
Saved presetsPlain text files ending in .partials. Drop one on the window to load it.
Not in this versionCopying pictures through the clipboard, copy / paste of line points, envelope state files, and the step-sequence editing window (there is a menu of ready-made sequences instead).
