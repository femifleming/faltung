faltung deep = faltung's dark mode

"Change the aesthetic of faltung deep to just be the dark mode of the faltung plugins/apps." Every faltung deep plug-in now has the layout, the type and the shapes of its faltung twin on the dark counterpart of the twin's colour field. Left: the faltung plug-in. Middle: faltung deep as it was. Right: faltung deep now. Sounds, parameters, presets, plug-in ids and saved sessions are unchanged.

the colour fields: light → dark (the field, with the slider fill on it)

One rule for all of them (ui::darkField / ui::darkAccent in Source/Ui.cpp): a field keeps its hue and goes to 8.5–17 % lightness — a saturated field keeps more of its colour, white paper goes near black with its tint; strips, lines and fills keep their hue and are pulled toward the middle so pale pastels dim and deep colours lift; ink turns pale; the white highlight stays white.
