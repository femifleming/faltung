faltung deep — four directions for the deep house suite

Each direction is shown as the kit drum machine and one small module. Colours change per plug-in in every option (the swatches under each show the set). Pick one — or a mix (e.g. A's layout with C's night palette).

A · mirror

After the Amphia sleeves: a mirrored photographic texture in one-colour duotone, the controls laid out like the back of the sleeve — artist / title top left, "Side A · Side B" columns, a catalogue number, tiny vertical credits on the right edge. Condensed DIN-style type. The texture slowly breathes with the sound; the mirror axis is the stereo centre.

B · mandala

After the first image: a slate field, a white line-drawn symmetric flower at the centre that opens, closes and turns with the sound (petals = voices, their length = level), and a soft green organic shape drifting behind. Thin white controls, lots of air.

C · night

After Codex EP: deep indigo, soft blurred light leaks mirrored left and right, film grain, pale pink type and controls. The light shapes swell with each hit — the most atmospheric of the four, made for late sessions.

D · perlon

After Perlon's sleeves: warm off-white paper, playful hand-cut shapes in a few bold colours, a slightly crooked, hand-made feel, rounded serif titles. Each voice is a paper cut-out that hops when it plays.
