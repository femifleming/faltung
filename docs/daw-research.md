# sndstudio — what the reference artists do, and which feature covers it

Compiled from interviews and articles (sources linked); where an artist does not discuss gear that is said rather
than guessed. The right-hand column names the sndstudio feature that covers the technique.

## cross-cutting, and where they live in sndstudio
1. non-isochronous and per-step-length sequencing → step `len`, speed, free clock, ratchets with free time
2. long-take capture with later excerpting → audio tracks, resample project, markers, split / trim
3. dub effects as instruments → throws, sends, mutes and solos as mappable performance controls, automation of all of it
4. resampling everywhere → resample track / bus / project to a new audio or sampler track
5. vocal micro-sampling with per-step pitch and mute groups → `st:sampler`
6. precision generators → `st:tone`, `st:data`, `st:noise`
7. tape, varispeed, detune → `st:vinyl`, `st:colour`, drift modulators, pitch locks
8. grid-free audio editing → snap off on the timeline
9. hardware integration → midi clock out, midi in to any track, audio inputs, hosted plug-ins
10. self mastering with side-length awareness → ms0 (auto as a starting point), bar / minute readout, markers
